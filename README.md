# CECS-101 Digital Portfolio

Welcome. This is my digital portfolio for **CECS-101: Design Your Degree, Design Your Career**.

## About Me

My name is Ian, and I am a student working at the intersection of two things I care about: building with technology and developing food and beverage products. A lot of my time goes into product formulation — figuring out how ingredients behave, why a flavor holds or fades, and what it takes to turn an idea into something a person can actually buy. I am increasingly interested in how AI and software can accelerate that kind of work.

Right now I am building a functional confectionery and beverage brand of my own. That project has taught me that making a product is only part of the job; costing, sourcing, scaling, and explaining it clearly matter just as much. I am using my degree to build the technical and professional skills that support all of it.

## About This Portfolio

This repository is the home base for my work in CECS-101. It collects the projects, reflections, and career exploration activities I complete during the course, organized so that they tell a coherent story rather than sitting in a folder somewhere.

If you are visiting: the folders listed below hold my coursework, and this README is the map. I will keep it current as the semester progresses.

## My Academic and Career Goals

**Academic goals**

- Build a strong foundation in computing and structured problem-solving that I can apply to real product work
- Learn to use AI tools deliberately, as leverage on hard problems rather than a shortcut past understanding them
- Finish the semester with a portfolio that shows evidence of learning, not just a list of completed assignments

**Career goals**

- Work at the intersection of food and beverage product development and applied technology
- Continue growing the functional confectionery and beverage brand I am building
- Build software that solves real problems for small producers, where costing, formulation, and scale-up are all underserved by existing tools
